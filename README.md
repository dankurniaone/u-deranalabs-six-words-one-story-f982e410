**Six Words, One Story** — an entry for make.fun's
[Six Words, One Story](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deranalabs-six-words-one-story-f982e410)
bounty ($5, top 3 share, 1st $2). One complete story in exactly six words,
on any subject; a judge model compares entries two at a time.

## The story

> He counted stars. She counted exits.

Six words, 36 characters. Two people, one sentence each: he looks up at the
stars, she looks for the door. The whole relationship — and its ending — is
implied, never spelled out. Every word earns its place: *counted* carries the
parallel, *stars* against *exits* is the turn, and the reader fills in what
happened between them.

## The program

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies) writes the story to `/output/entry.txt`
and self-checks the sealed gate before writing: exactly six words, 1–60
characters, not only whitespace. Run it with `node main.ts`.
